# TECHNICAL INSTITUTION REPRESENTATIVE MANUAL
## AICTE Smart Approval & Compliance Management System

---

## 1. Institution Portal Overview
Technical institutions interact with the Council through the **Institution Portal**:
- Representative accounts are pre-bound to their specific institution (e.g. `inst_apex` for Apex Institute, `inst_southern` for Southern National College).
- Institution Representatives can only view and manage their own institution's records.

## 2. Submitting an Approval Application
1. Switch to an **Institution Representative** role (or log in with institution credentials).
2. Navigate to **Applications** and click **+ Submit New Application**.
3. Select the applicable Application Type:
   - Setting Up New Technical Institution (`NEW_INST`)
   - Extension of Approval (`EOA`)
   - Increase in Intake / Additional Course (`INC_INTAKE`)
4. Confirm submission. The application is assigned a unique Application Number (`AICTE-2026-XXXXX`) and routed to the Scrutiny stage.

## 3. Responding to Scrutiny Deficiencies
1. If the Scrutiny Panel flags a deficiency, the case status will reflect `DEFICIENCY_RAISED`.
2. Open the application workspace and click the **Scrutiny** tab.
3. Locate the deficiency item and click **Submit Response**.
4. Enter the detailed compliance clarification and attach supporting documentary evidence.
5. Click **Submit Response**. The application status updates to `RESPONSES_SUBMITTED`, notifying the scrutiny committee for re-scrutiny.

## 4. Submitting Evidence for Time-Bound Undertakings
1. Navigate to **Undertakings Register**.
2. Locate the conditional undertaking (e.g., equipment installation, library expansion).
3. Click **Submit Evidence**.
4. Provide the evidence title, document URL, and completion details.
5. Notice that the status transitions to `UNDER_VERIFICATION`. Final statutory compliance is granted only after a Compliance Officer reviews and approves the evidence.

## 5. Renewing Expiring Land Lease Deeds
1. Navigate to **Lease & Validity (90d)**.
2. Documents nearing expiration are highlighted with countdown days.
3. Click **Submit Renewal**.
4. Input the new registered lease dates and upload the attested supplementary deed.
5. The submission enters `RENEWAL_SUBMITTED` status until formally verified by Council officers.
